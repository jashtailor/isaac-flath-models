# Isaac Flath — Models

Everything Isaac Flath says about PDF and OCR models, collected from his Instagram posts.

This repo is the models-only companion to [jashtailor/pdf-processing-skill](https://github.com/jashtailor/pdf-processing-skill). That repo holds the full skill. This repo holds only the model talk: what each model is, what Isaac tested it on, what it did well, and what it got wrong.

## Contents

- `MODELS.md` — the detailed report on every model Isaac talks about. Tested in his posts vs named only on the market list, clearly marked.
- `models/` — per-model notes, as they grow.

## How it is updated

Updated weekly, at the same time as the regular Isaac Flath sync (Monday 9:00 AM IST). New posts are checked for model talk, and this repo is updated alongside `pdf-processing-skill`.

## Caveats

Every test Isaac shows is a one-to-three-page demo. Claims rest on captions plus automated visual descriptions of his reels. 30 of 175 posts are caption-only. Use these notes as leads to test on your own pages, never as a vendor ranking.
